# Afrigen — Ready-to-Paste Prompt Library

40+ prompts you can copy straight into Afrigen, grouped by niche. Use them for your
own content **and** repackage them as "type this → get this" posts (see the
[30-day calendar](30-day-content-calendar.md)).

## Tips before you paste

- Pick the right **style** in the app: **African** for local scenes, **Social Media**
  for vertical ad-style clips, **Cinematic** for premium/product looks.
- The 4 anchors of a good prompt: **who · where · what · how it feels**.
- Always name a **real place** and a **time of day / light** — that's what stops a
  clip looking generic and "foreign".
- These are starting points. Tweak the place, product, and mood to fit you.

## Turn any prompt into a post

1. Screen-record yourself typing the prompt into Afrigen.
2. Cut to the generated clip.
3. Caption: a hook + "Make yours free 👉 [your link]".
4. Pin the link as the first comment too.
