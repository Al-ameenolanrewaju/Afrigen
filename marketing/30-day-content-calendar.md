# Afrigen — 30-Day Content Calendar

Goal: get real users by showing the product's output daily, with a strong hook and
a clear "where do I get this" CTA every time. The product makes the content that
markets the product — that's the whole loop.

## How to use this

- **Post once a day minimum.** TikTok is priority #1, then Reels, then X. Repost
  every clip to YouTube Shorts + Facebook Status with zero extra effort.
- **Each clip is made *in Afrigen*** using the prompt given. Screen-record the
  prompt → cut to the result for the "type this → get this" days.
- **Every post needs the CTA:** Afrigen link in bio, in the caption, AND pinned as
  the first comment. A viral video with no "where do I get this" is wasted reach.
- **First 1–2 seconds decide everything.** Open with motion or the finished clip,
  never a talking-head intro.
- **Track signups, not views.** A clip with 50k views + 200 signups beats one with
  1M views + 3 signups. Make more of whatever converts.

## Format key

- **TTGT** — "Type this → get this" (show prompt, cut to result). Your strongest format.
- **COST** — Naira cost comparison (agency/videographer vs. Afrigen credits).
- **AUDIENCE** — Make an ad for a follower's business (pulled from comments/DMs).
- **LOCAL** — "AI tries to make [Nigerian thing]" — comedy/relatable.
- **BA** — Before/after for a small business.
- **TREND** — Recreate the week's trending sound/format with AI.
- **EDU** — Quick teaching post (prompt tips, carousels) — builds authority + saves.

## Repeatable weekly skeleton (use after Day 30)

| Day | Format | Why |
|-----|--------|-----|
| Mon | TTGT | Strongest converter — start the week strong |
| Tue | COST | Naira angle drives shares |
| Wed | AUDIENCE | Tagged followers reshare = free reach |
| Thu | LOCAL | Comedy/relatable travels furthest |
| Fri | BA or TREND | Proof, or ride the week's trend |
| Sat | EDU | Builds authority + gets saved |
| Sun | Best-of repost | Recycle the week's winner to a new platform |

## Niches to keep rotating through

Food/restaurants · fashion & thrift · skincare/beauty · real estate · gadgets/phones ·
events & catering · logistics/dispatch · makeup artists · fitness · gospel/church ·
travel/tourism · car dealers · interior decor · agriculture · fintech/small SaaS.

## Measure weekly

- Signups per post (the real number).
- Which **format** and which **niche** convert best → do more of those.
- Comments asking "how did you make this?" = the hook is working.
- Cut anything that gets views but no signups, however fun it is.
