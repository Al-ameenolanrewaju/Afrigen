Install Afrigen on Your Phone

Enjoy a native mobile app experience with zero app store downloads. Generate AI videos, prompts, and content directly from your home screen.

Ready to Install

Your browser supports one-click install. Tap below to add Afrigen to your device.

Android (Chrome)

Samsung, Google Pixel, Tecno, Infinix, Xiaomi
  1. Open Chrome and go to afrigen.com.ng
  2. Tap the three dots (⋮) menu in the top-right corner.
  3. Tap "Install app" or "Add to Home screen".
  4. Confirm by tapping "Install". Afrigen will appear on your app drawer and home screen.

iPhone & iPad (Safari)

iOS 14.0 or newer
  1. Open Safari and navigate to afrigen.com.ng
  2. Tap the Share button at the bottom of Safari.
  3. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen" .
  4. Tap "Add" in the top-right corner. The Afrigen icon will appear on your iPhone screen!
