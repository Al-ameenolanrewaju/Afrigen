🔒 Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Afrigen ("we", "us", or "our"), owned and operated by Al-ameen Olanrewaju, collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you use our platform at afrigen.com. We are committed to protecting your privacy in compliance with the Nigeria Data Protection Regulation (NDPR) and applicable international data protection laws.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following information when you use Afrigen:

  • Account Information — username, email address, and password (encrypted) when you register
  • Google OAuth Data — name, email, and profile picture if you sign in with Google
  • Payment Information — processed securely by Paystack; we do not store your card details
  • Usage Data — prompts you enter, videos and images you generate, generation history
  • Technical Data — IP address, country, browser type, device information
  • Referral Data — referral codes used and referrals made
  • Source Data — how you found Afrigen (e.g. Facebook, Google, direct)
3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and improve the Afrigen platform
  • Process payments and manage your subscription
  • Send you account-related emails (welcome, generation complete, upgrade confirmation)
  • Track and reward referrals
  • Analyze platform usage to improve our services
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, and violations of our Terms of Service
  • Comply with legal obligations
4. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with:

  • Paystack — for secure payment processing
  • fal.ai — your prompts are sent to generate videos and images
  • Groq — your prompts are processed by AI to refine them
  • ElevenLabs — scripts are sent for AI voiceover generation (Pro users only)
  • Google — if you use Google OAuth to sign in

All third-party services are bound by their own privacy policies and data protection agreements.

5. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely on our servers. We use industry-standard encryption for passwords and sensitive data. Payment information is handled entirely by Paystack and never stored on our servers. We take reasonable measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, but no system is 100% secure.

6. Cookies

Afrigen uses cookies and session storage to keep you logged in and remember your preferences. We do not use tracking cookies for advertising. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, but this may affect the functionality of the platform.

7. Your Rights

Under the NDPR and applicable laws, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and data
  • Object to processing of your data
  • Data portability — receive your data in a readable format

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at afrigenng@gmail.com.

8. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we will remove your personal data within 30 days. Generated content hosted on third-party servers may persist according to their own retention policies.

9. Children's Privacy

Afrigen is not intended for users under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us immediately at afrigenng@gmail.com.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes via email. Continued use of the platform after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
