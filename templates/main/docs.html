Campaigns & Auto-Publishing

Automate your entire content creation and social media strategy with Campaigns!

How Campaigns Work

Instead of generating one post at a time, you can create a Campaign. Give the AI a topic (like "Weekly African Business News"), and it will automatically generate a suite of assets including:

  • Facebook Posts (with generated images)
  • LinkedIn Articles
  • X (Twitter) Threads
  • Videos and Images tailored to the campaign theme
Auto-Publishing to Social Media

You no longer have to manually copy and paste your generated content! Afrigen can automatically publish your campaign assets to your linked social media accounts the moment they are generated.

How to set it up:
1. Go to your Dashboard and navigate to Connected Accounts.
2. Connect your Facebook Page, LinkedIn, or X (Twitter) account.
3. Start a new Campaign! Once the AI finishes generating a post for a connected platform, it will be added to the Publishing Queue and posted automatically.

Resilient Publishing Queue

If a social media platform (like Facebook or X) happens to be temporarily down when your post is ready, don't worry! Afrigen uses a robust background queue that will silently retry publishing your post up to 5 times until it succeeds.

❓ FAQ

How long does video generation take?

Video generation typically takes 30-90 seconds depending on the API used.

Why did my video generation fail?

Video APIs occasionally fail due to high demand. Simply try again and our fallback system will use a different API automatically.

Can I use generated videos commercially?

Pro plan users can use videos for commercial purposes. Free plan videos are for personal use only.

How do I upgrade to Pro?

Click the "Go Pro" button in your dashboard or navbar. We accept payments via Paystack (cards, bank transfer, USSD).

Do credits roll over?

Free plan limits are a one-time trial and do not reset. You are limited to 1 video and 2 photos total.

How do I contact support?

Email us at contact@afrigen.com.ng or use our Contact Page. We respond within 24 hours!
