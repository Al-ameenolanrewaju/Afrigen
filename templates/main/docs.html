🚀 Getting Started

Welcome to Afrigen — the AI video generation platform built for African creators!

Step 1 — Create Your Account

Click Sign Up and create a free account. You get 5 free credits immediately!

Step 2 — Go to Dashboard

After logging in, visit your dashboard to start generating content.

Step 3 — Generate Your First Video

Type a simple idea like "A Nigerian businessman in Lagos" and click Generate! Our AI will refine your prompt and generate a stunning video.

Tips for Better Videos
  • Be specific about location (Lagos, Abuja, etc.)

❓ FAQ

How long does video generation take?

Video generation typically takes 30-90 seconds depending on the API used.

Why did my video generation fail?

Video APIs occasionally fail due to high demand. Simply try again and our fallback system will use a different API automatically.

Can I use generated videos commercially?

Pro plan users can use videos for commercial purposes. Free plan videos are for personal use only.

How do I upgrade to Pro?

Click the "Go Pro" button in your dashboard or navbar. We accept payments via Paystack (cards, bank transfer, USSD).

Do credits roll over?

Free plan credits reset on the 1st of every month and do not roll over. Pro plan users have unlimited credits so rollover doesn't apply.

How do I contact support?

Email us at afrigenng@gmail.com or use our Contact Page. We respond within 24 hours!
